## Why

The banked J9 lookup reduces chemistry cost while retaining the
reference factorization, shape and 12-bit angles. Further lookup
changes alone leave the preparation cost largely intact. A classical
factorization and precision screen can identify candidates with a
lower complete block-encoding cost before native synthesis.

## Scope

- Reproduce the reference J8 and certified banked J9 chemistry controls.
- Build a bounded weight-only pilot, then screen a small shape,
  precision and tree design space with the pinned numerical model.
- Generate and compare width-safe XOR loader layouts for a short
  list of already feasible decoded word tables.
- Verify and independently confirm the complete changed chemistry
  scope, including the NPZ and tree. Use the
  [shared coverage and release plan](../prepare-next-hybrid-verification/proposal.md)
  before promotion of any changed shape.
