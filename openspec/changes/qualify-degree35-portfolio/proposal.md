## Why

The degree-35 decoder effort now targets a complete aggregate of at least
35. The selected public controls give measured component costs for
this target, and the release tools need a registered threshold that binds
the same two independent builds and verifier roles.

## What changes

- Add a versioned inclusive score-35 registration and exact cost-product acceptance.
- Freeze the open source controls for degree-35 development and compute
  strict DQI gate limits from both sets of companion costs.
- Qualify a complete decoder and the independently built portfolio before
  delivering the submission archive.

## Capabilities

### New capabilities

- `degree35-portfolio` covers the source-bound degree-35 release at an
  aggregate of at least 35.

## Impact

This change affects the portfolio preparation and two-role audit. The
existing above-20, above-34, strict above-35, and above-36 tiers keep their
existing score semantics.
