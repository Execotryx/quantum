## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reproduce open decoder candidates with established provenance

The screen SHALL verify the source and downstream license scope, pin the
revision and input hashes, apply the project's vendoring exclusions and
bind two independent byte-identical builds to the official register
contract and backend. Imported source and independently produced code
SHALL have distinct provenance records.

#### Scenario: An artifact is public but the build is unavailable

- **WHEN** a candidate is available but its generating source or lawful lineage cannot be verified
- **THEN** the intake records the exact missing evidence and excludes its bytes from replacement qualification

#### Scenario: Regenerating an admissible candidate

- **WHEN** two clean builds run from the pinned source and dependency environment
- **THEN** both produce the same KMB bytes, agree with any pinned published hash and pass the official register validator

### Requirement: Reproduce exact phase and cleanup on recorded D4 regressions

The screen SHALL replay the known historical D4 phase and value witnesses,
their passing controls and representative pole and terminating cases on
the complete emitted circuit. It SHALL retain independent scalar checks
and record syndrome restoration, mask preservation, scratch cleanup and
phase in each result.

#### Scenario: A changed readout consumes an intermediate by measurement

- **WHEN** the serialized stage and linked circuit run with alternate masks and measurement outcomes
- **THEN** the phase agrees with the oracle, the live boundaries meet the interface and all inputs and scratch meet their final restoration contract
- **AND** an intentionally broken phase repayment produces the expected diagnostic failure

### Requirement: Gate official pricing on an economic native preflight

The screen SHALL report declared width, peak live allocation, static
operations and executed Toffoli-like mean. It SHALL apply the pinned
eight-draw minimum repetition factor to whole-circuit preflight results
and record the registered threshold and decision before official pricing.

#### Scenario: The candidate exceeds its width-specific price ceiling

- **WHEN** the registered preflight gives no resource headroom at the measured width
- **THEN** the screen records the candidate and evidence as a no-go for official pricing

### Requirement: Qualify a successor by a complete fresh paired comparison

The campaign SHALL use the frozen candidate and banked artifacts, eight
fresh preregistered salts and 102,400 shots per role and salt. It SHALL
retain original circuit and backend identities and compute disjoint errors
and pooled price from the full receipts.

#### Scenario: Selecting the DQI component

- **WHEN** both roles finish the complete campaign
- **THEN** a replacement is eligible only if every draw for both roles passes, the historical regression gate passed and its pooled price is strictly lower than the banked role's
- **AND** any release handoff binds the exact selected bytes and requires separate confirmation and extracted-archive acceptance

### Requirement: Verify a combined aggregate above twenty

The aggregate qualification SHALL use the frozen, reproducible inverse
square root and chemistry components with the qualified DQI member.
Separate fresh archive extractions and salts SHALL receive full
verification using the pinned published metric.

#### Scenario: Checking the exact combined score

- **WHEN** the complete metric and confirmation receipts bind the same archive and its original members
- **THEN** each result must have full official coverage and a geometric-mean aggregate strictly greater than 20
