## Why

Banked D4 remains the selected 897-qubit DQI member. Its historical
repair-campaign control cost 414,840,001,725.70 across eight official
draws. The full-grid repair cleared the four archived phase failures
but introduced two verifier-visible value errors in fresh draws and cost
153.67 times as much. The guarded regular, adjacent and separated
compact probes use 1,011–1,503 qubits and exceed the historical resource
screen. Publicly released circuits provide another path to evaluate.

## What Changes

- Screen a short list of reproducible open decoders against banked D4.
- Use native boundary measurements to choose at most one exact
  readout or cleanup optimization on the strongest admissible base.
- Require matched historical regression coverage, economic preflight
  and a fresh paired official campaign before replacing D4.
- Record a selected successor or a no-go decision with the evidence
  needed to reproduce it.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `dqi-open-d4-successor` covers reproducible public-decoder screening,
  exact circuit optimization and replacement qualification.

## Impact

Implementation can extend source admission, candidate build adapters,
DQI replay and registration tooling, and evidence. A successor changes
only the DQI member through the existing certification workflow. Release
and submission are separate decisions.
