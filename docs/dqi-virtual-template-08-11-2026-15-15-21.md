# Exact virtual-template BM schedule

For a correctable input, write the routed template as `tau=z^age*base`, where `base[0] != 0`. Initially `base=1`, `age=0`. Zero-discrepancy and retain rounds increment `age` without moving or truncating `base`; replace stores the old locator as `base` and resets `age` to zero. The counter and discrepancy history follow the rescaled routed recurrence. At 70 rounds, age is in 0–70 and needs seven bits. Both the locator and saved base fit 36 GF(4096) words if the correctable-domain locator bound below holds.

For an error vector of weight at most `t=35`, its true locator of degree at most `t` is a valid recurrence for every syndrome prefix; the BM minimal-recurrence property therefore bounds every intermediate locator degree by `min(r,t)`. This is the exact-domain property used by the [source's worst-case schedule](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22367v1). On a nonzero update the multiplier `alpha` (or `alpha^-1` on replace) is nonzero. If the shifted template had a nonzero coefficient above `t`, the old locator, which has no such coefficient, could not cancel it; the new locator would exceed degree `t`, a contradiction. Thus every active update uses only coefficients through `t`. This does not allow the zero-discrepancy path to truncate a materialized template: its virtual age can reach 70 while its base stays `1`.

To reverse a replace, first recover the old shifted template from the output locator and old locator using the routed inverse. It is nonzero and divisible by `z`. Because `base[0] != 0`, its first nonzero coefficient uniquely determines `age+1`; divide by that power of `z` to restore `base` and `age`. Retain and zero rounds undo the age increment. A coherent first-nonzero search and its scratch cleanup are still to be designed and measured; the classical inverse is not that quantum circuit.

Let `Z` count zero-discrepancy rounds and `R` nonzero retain rounds. The invariant `Z-R >= |d|` starts at zero; a zero raises the left side by one and changes `|d|` by at most one, a retain decreases both sides by one because `d<0`, and a replace preserves `|d|`. Hence `R<=Z`. If `R>0`, at least one replace is required to make the initially zero counter negative. Thus `r>=2R+1`, so `R<=floor((r-1)/2)`: at most **34** retained discrepancies in 70 rounds. The bound is tight for unrestricted GF(4096) syndromes: a tested construction forces 34 zero discrepancies, a replace, 34 retains, then one zero. That witness is not claimed to be in the correctable domain.

| History layout | Conservative persistent bits | Unmeasured work |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Static per-round slots | `70*(1+12) = 910` | Clearing branch and discrepancy records |
| Indexed stack | `34*(7+12)+6+70 = 722` | Controlled push/pop, pointer and index comparison, branch clearing |

The indexed stack includes a 7-bit round index per entry, six pointer bits, and 70 replace flags; the flags are retained because their recovery has not been proved. Excluding all arithmetic, routing and cleanup scratch, the syndrome bank, two 36-word rows, seven-bit age, eight-bit counter and history require at least 2,629 qubits with slots or 2,441 with the stack. At those lower-bound widths and equal remaining work, the stack can add up to about 16.00% Toffoli cost before losing its squared-width advantage. Actual workspace and gate counts are needed before selecting a layout.

All 23,851 correctable GF(16), `n=4`, `t=2` syndromes were checked round-by-round against the unrestricted routed recurrence, including reverse maps; observed maximum ages were `[1,2,3,4]` and retained counts `[0,0,1,1]`. One GF(4096) case at every supported weight 0–35 also matched the independent locator and restored initialization. The invalid prefix `[0,0,1,0]` at radius two fails closed when its active shift exceeds the radius. A reduced native three-word virtual-shift lookup, with coherent age and enable and caller-enforced support precondition, costs 52 Toffoli-like gates at 80 declared qubits and passes XOR-target, phase, scratch and composition checks. It is not a complete BM round. Its direct full-width controlled-copy network would use 7,560 CCX gates per lookup before equality tests or cleanup, so it is a structural fixture rather than a viable unpriced schedule. Receipts and per-round bounds are in `docs/evidence/dqi-virtual-bm-20261108/`. Forty WSL tests, Ruff and Pyright passed; full round emission, stack-versus-slot selection, full-size cost and decoder admission remain open.
