# Research brief for a verified aggregate above 36

## Question and evidence

Which source-reproducible changes can plausibly reduce the combined benchmark cost enough to exceed 36 on two complete verifier runs? This targeted review uses the host specification and metric, primary algorithm papers, exact public builds, and local development receipts. Literature supports candidate mechanisms. The pinned verifier supplies acceptance and cost evidence.

The public reference passed one full cloud run at **33.20483975**. The project archive passed its registered metric and confirmation at **26.78302388** and **26.78303192**. The public result supplies the stronger development control and the project archive supplies the certified release comparison. The pinned baseline product divided by 36³ is **3.650426 × 10²⁴**. The measured reference cost product must decrease by **21.53%** to cross that threshold. With both companion costs fixed, the one-component limits are **47,809.13** for inverse square root, **240.455B** for DQI, and **195.520M** for chemistry. A balanced planning allocation of **56,000 × 280B × 220M** projects **36.68547**. These are cost budgets and need three actual circuits. [Registered baseline constants](evidence/aggregate34-20261126/public-cloud-registration.json) · [Published scoring contract](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/google-verifiable-quantum-circuit-compilation/)

If chemistry stays at **249.170M** and inverse square root reaches **56,000**, DQI must fall below **261.613B**. At the first ceiling, `(8,127,42)` can spend 3,235 loader Toffolis per pass at the public normalization and modeled width. `(8,120,40)` can spend 3,313. The allocation therefore supports cheaper tests of fit and retained loaders before a new loader synthesis campaign.

Concentrate on source-level changes to the quotient, phase contraction, and return schedule. At the measured normalization factor of 46.9143037, the current 405-qubit shape needs at most 2,539 ANGLE Toffolis per pass to fit 220M, down from 3,307. At `(8,120,40)`, the 404-qubit and 5,657-fixed-gate model admits about **2,975** loader Toffolis per pass at the reference normalization. This numerical rank screen gives no inexpensive zero-column reduction or bound on a new variable-basis fit.

## Inverse-square-root arithmetic with joint phase budgets

At 35 qubits, 56,000 permits **1,600** charged equivalents per input, down about **2.84%** from the measured 37-qubit reference. At 34 qubits it permits **1,647.06**, essentially the reference arithmetic budget. Use a reproducible generator and profile the live multiplication and normalization stages before choosing an emission schedule. Test an early-uncompute or recompute schedule and a separately compiled segmented polynomial with quantized coefficient widths and late phase accumulation. Compare predicted width, gate count, retained phase margin, and actual synthesis charge before running a full verifier. The legal domain and every measurement-dependent phase repair are part of acceptance. [Piecewise arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12445) · [Measurement-based cleanup](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02345) · [Native affine-rank and rotation evidence](above-34-arithmetic-and-storage-screens-26-11-2026-06-18-11.md)

The exact 37-qubit reference generator remains unresolved. One source-complete portfolio has been reproduced twice, including its 39-qubit inverse-square-root circuit. The compiled-only 37-qubit reference can calibrate the numerical and resource targets. Promotion needs independently reproduced source lineage and native measurements. [Source intake and builds](aggregate-above-34-implementation-26-11-2026-05-40-22.md)

A finite accuracy pilot can normalize each positive integer as `x=4^k y` with `1≤y<4`. For equal-width dyadic intervals of width `h`, use a centered expansion in `t=(y-c)/c`, where `c` is the interval midpoint. On all intervals, `|t|≤ρ=h/(2+h)`. For degree `d`, the first omitted binomial term and the decreasing coefficient magnitudes give the absolute relative remainder bound `sqrt(1+ρ) |binom(-1/2,d+1)| ρ^(d+1)/(1-ρ)`. Degree four with 48 intervals of width 1/16 gives a bound below **6.59 × 10⁻⁹**. Degree three with 96 intervals of width 1/32 gives a bound below **1.57 × 10⁻⁸**. These bounds cover real-arithmetic truncation. Coefficient rounding, input normalization, reversible arithmetic, phase synthesis, and register cleanup have independent cost or error obligations. The 22 fixed input bits leave 12–13 scratch bits at widths 34–35. A separate 24-bit accumulator would already require 46 total qubits before selector and product scratch. A streamed or input-lending schedule must show a constructive live-interval budget and include its recomputation cost.
