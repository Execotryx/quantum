# Public inverse-square-root source decision

The three registered candidates matched their published SHA-256 hashes.
Each depends on a precompiled circuit with no complete generator at the
registered source locations. The screen ended at admission with `no_go`
and the project retained confirmed v6 inverse square root. The
[admission record](evidence/vendored-invsqrt/admission.md) captures the
origin, revision, license and missing generation step for each candidate.
