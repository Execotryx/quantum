# D4 open decoder successor plan

Added the OpenSpec change
[`screen-open-dqi-d4-successor`](../openspec/changes/screen-open-dqi-d4-successor/proposal.md)
to evaluate a lower-cost DQI replacement for banked D4.

Astra inspected Syed Asad Ali's v12 DQI builder and found a published
889-qubit build with source and official backend pins matching this
checkout. The plan gives this candidate priority and pins the source
and output identities. The reported 360.73 billion cost is an upstream
result. Project qualification requires independent byte-identical
builds, exact historical and generated pole regressions, a full native
resource screen and paired official pooled pricing. The guarded compact
paths remain differential controls because their measured executions
exceed the historical width-adjusted ceilings.

The change adds a proposal, design, implementation checklist and
acceptance scenarios. Candidate implementation and selection are
pending.
