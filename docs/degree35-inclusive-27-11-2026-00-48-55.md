# Degree-35 acceptance and control screening

The degree-35 portfolio accepts an aggregate of **35 or higher**. The
new `portfolio-v5` registration compares the product of the measured
costs with the exact score-35 limit in each full verifier role. It accepts
equality even when the displayed aggregate rounds below 35. Existing
`portfolio-v4` registrations still require strictly above 35.
[Degree-35 OpenSpec](../openspec/changes/qualify-degree35-portfolio/specs/degree35-portfolio/spec.md)

The highest complete cloud comparison is **33.20483975**. The best
existing public mix projects **33.22945**. With the source-rebuilt
companions, the 888-qubit decoder has a budget of approximately
**274,327.64 mean gates** at zero failures. The supplied-tape phase
already uses **253,851 explicit ANDs** and 202 equality calls before
native lowering. The remaining **20,476.64 gates** amount to at most 243
rank-42 field multiplications per direction if all available gates go
to extraction and its inverse. The measured prefix and suffix pair
already costs 127,080 gates round trip. A full raw-syndrome decoder and
its exceptional-chart recovery remain open.

## Inverse-square-root rotation screen

Tony Li's 39-qubit source-built control was replayed on every 3,133,452
legal input on the all-zero measurement branch. The registered screen
keeps the existing control bits and allows signed coefficient offsets
of up to `20 * 2^32` phase64 units. It checks input preservation and
scratch cleanup on that branch. A weighted sum of integer inequalities
bounds the best achievable minimum margin. The original margin was
23,944,050 phase64 units and the refit upper bound was 23,944,061.

| Removed assembly line | Upper bound on minimum margin in phase64 units |
| --- | ---: |
| 10022 | -86,390,782 |
| 9979 | -62,306,115 |
| 9937 | -346,328,810 |
| 9902 | -978,153,748 |

The weighted constraints for line 9979 cancel all editable
coefficients, so that upper bound does not depend on the offset box
within the linear phase model. Each negative bound rules out that
removal in the registered refit, since full verification also has
to pass this branch. The five bounds and the registration and driver
hashes were recomputed independently from the saved rows.

## Raw-syndrome research

The rational-reconstruction reduction by Neiger, Safey El Din, and Tran
connects Padé reconstruction to relation bases at a chosen modulus.
Its Cauchy interpolation route is a candidate for constructing a
locator from raw moments. The in-place division schedules of Giorgi,
Grenet, and Roche provide coefficient buffer layouts to compare. These
are classical results. A relevant circuit screen would count live
field words and charge both directions including pivot and scale
recovery, with degree drops and repeated-root cases in the fixture
set. The complete 888-qubit budget allows at most 20,476.64 additional
gates before pricing the remaining comparisons. A prototype needs to
reduce the supplied-tape cost as well if it exceeds that envelope.
