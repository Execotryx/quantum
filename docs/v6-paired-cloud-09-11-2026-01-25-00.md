# v6 hybrid paired cloud verification

Two independent builds produced the same archive SHA-256,
`5eb18b89d67c8e8593e9acf492727beb13a01f6a2dd67815cfda7dfda303578d`.
The pinned cloud metric passed on a fresh extraction with full coverage
for all three benchmarks. The v6 archive scored **1.7339568375**,
**4.70%** above the v5 score of **1.6561824298**.

| Benchmark | v5 cost | v6 cost | Full coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Inverse square root | 472,336.9178780867 | 411,586.9178780867 | 200,540,928 trajectories |
| DQI | 24,439,466,510,149.91 | 24,439,466,510,149.91 | 102,400 trajectories |
| Chemistry | 3,247,752,448.668952 | 3,247,752,448.668952 | 254,464 checks |

The comparison retains the exact v5 DQI and chemistry member hashes
and reproduces their costs and the DQI error counters. The independent
full and fresh inverse-square-root receipts bind to the same frozen v6
circuit as the archive. The paired request review identified only a
project-root path change and preserved both backend identities.
