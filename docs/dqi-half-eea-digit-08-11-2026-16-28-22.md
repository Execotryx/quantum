# Coherent half-degree EEA quotient digit

The reduced native slice takes selected leading numerator and denominator coefficients from three-word remainder rows. It computes the selected quotient into an initially zero 12-bit history word using the clean subtower inverse and field product, applies the shifted monomial update to the remainder and both Bézout coefficient row pairs, and conditionally exchanges the row pairs. Its separately emitted inverse undoes the exchange and update, recomputes the quotient from the restored leading words, and clears quotient history and scratch.

If the selected denominator is zero, a coherent flag is set, the quotient is zero, and the rows are unchanged; the inverse clears the flag. A zero selected coefficient can mean the supplied degree is wrong, not necessarily that the entire divisor polynomial is zero. The caller must establish that the selected coefficients are the actual leading words and that all shifted terms beyond the row width vanish. The slice does not emit those checks. It swaps after this digit, so it represents a complete Euclidean step only when the full quotient is a monomial. General multi-digit quotient handling, dynamic degree selection, variable-length history, half-degree EEA and reciprocal assembly remain open.

Retaining the quotient is necessary for this generic slice unless some additional input-domain reconstruction is proved: for example, a leading numerator `a*z^2` divided by `z` loses `a` when the leading term is eliminated. This observation does not rule out recovery from additional invariants in a specialized full EEA.

The three-word, shift-one circuit uses 702 Toffoli-like gates and 280 declared qubits in each direction: 204 for two clean inversions, 54 for quotient formation, 324 for six field-product updates, 108 controlled swaps, and 12 for the zero flag. For an 18-word version of this fixed slice, the corresponding structural count is `270+3*18*12+3*(18-shift)*54`, or `3834-162*shift` per direction; this excludes dynamic degree selection, checking, and history addressing and is not a full EEA cost. A selected quotient plus zero flag occupies 13 persistent bits until the inverse; a dynamic shift would need its own record or a proof of recovery.

The native test covers nonzero selected denominator, zero numerator, zero denominator, randomized row words, phase, scratch and exact reversal on 128 shots. Focused WSL testing, Ruff and Pyright passed. Forward and reverse circuit receipts are in `docs/evidence/dqi-half-digit-20261108/`. This is a quotient-digit primitive, not a full reciprocal or DQI admission.
