# Bounded GF(64) rank search

Added a seeded flip-graph walk from the existing 18-term GF(64) multiplication tensor. A flip changes two tensor terms sharing exactly one factor while preserving their sum in characteristic two. When the current decomposition has no legal flip, the search adds a cancelling pair, bounded to two extra terms; it periodically merges terms sharing two factors. Limits, seed, restart interval, initial tensor identity and source hashes are registered before each run. The expanded runs also retain exact runner, search, field and tower source bytes beside their receipts.

Every accepted improvement is checked on the 36 input basis pairs. Initial and final tensors are checked on all 4,096 GF(64) input pairs against the pinned GF(4096) polynomial-field arithmetic and the registered six-word subfield basis. Tests cover 3,000 tensor-preserving transformations, a mutated tensor, deterministic step-limited repetition and invalid limits. Three focused WSL tests, Ruff and Pyright passed.

The first registration (`dqi-rank-search-20261108`) exposed a search limitation: the original 18 terms share no factors, so the basic walk made zero moves. The initial search-source hash is retained, but its exact source snapshot was not captured; treat that result as a diagnostic rather than a reproducible experiment. The expansion-assisted 100,000-step registration (`dqi-rank-search-expanded-20261108`) made 99,949 flips and retained rank 18. A separately registered 1,000,000-step run (`dqi-rank-search-long-20261108`) made 999,641 flips, took 33.58 seconds, and also retained rank 18. Both final rank-18 decompositions passed exhaustive verification. Neither search establishes that rank 15 is impossible, and neither supplies the targeted rank-15 tensor or a 45-gate multiplier. The corresponding OpenSpec task remains open.
