# Evidence for a portfolio above 34 points

## Question and method

Which reproducible circuit changes can reduce the product of the three verified costs enough to exceed 34 on distinct full-strength draws? This focused Academic Research Suite brief used the official scoring contract, matched local certificates, original notebook execution logs, and primary papers. It grouped observations by circuit lineage so repackaged copies supply a single measurement. The search covered Kaggle's competition and public notebooks, author repositories, and arXiv. It used the latest accessible notebook output on 26 November 2026.

## Candidate routes

- **Inverse square root.** The public 33.25226 notebook regenerates the small phase rewrite from a pinned compiled parent, including a three-T to two-S rewrite, and reports 1,406.31 executed Toffolis, 5.50 T gates per trajectory, 33 rotations and 1.888e-13 maximum phase error. It imports part of the lineage as compiled bytes. Trace the parent back to a complete generator or build an independent direct-phasing generator. Jointly search peak live width, address decoding and shared polynomial products. Keep row-specific rotation counts and mathematical phase margin in the objective. Prove changed phase predicates and scratch on the full legal domain and for all measurement branches. [Original notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/goodpjw2008/qcc-shorter-chemistry-loaders-lb-33-25226?scriptVersionId=374068672) · [Competition companion paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23832)
- **DQI.** The public 900-qubit circuit has about 316,123 executed Toffolis. Its ten terminal rewrites have marginal impact on whole-circuit cost. At the observed four-error multiplier, Q=880 needs roughly 309.6k executed Toffolis to reach 290B. Holding Q=900 requires roughly 296k. A bounded search should profile forward and reverse EEA, basis conversion and scan separately, then test live-range sharing and reversible quotient or product reuse on the largest compatible stages. The primary DQI paper derives a leading-order EEA width of two polynomial banks. The 840 syndrome input qubits constrain how much additional width can be removed. Reproduce pole, termination and low-weight fixtures and measure fresh matched salts before claiming savings from decoder changes. [Ablations and floors](https://www.kaggle.com/code/asteriospanoras/qcc-33-1933-ablation-floors-human-vs-ai-next) · [Khattar et al., v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10967v2)
- **Chemistry.** The notebook reports a passing factorization with shape (9, 127, 46), 3,309 ANGLE Toffolis per pass, 13,118 total Toffolis and lambda_eff 46.91430. At the same total width and lambda_eff, 245M requires at most 12,894 total Toffolis, or up to 3,197 ANGLE Toffolis per pass at the observed 6,500 fixed cost. Search sparse coefficient support, Givens tree order and shared product synthesis together on the legal loader endpoints. Preserve proof of phase and scratch clearing, then recompute both Hamiltonian residuals and gap after decoding. The notebook's residuals are within about 1.2e-10 Ha of their upper limits and its gap is within about 1.7e-11 Ha of the lower limit, so refits need explicit headroom and checks on the exact packaged precision. [Original notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/goodpjw2008/qcc-shorter-chemistry-loaders-lb-33-25226?scriptVersionId=374068672) · [Low et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15882)

The public notebook logs show that the circuit designs can achieve near-target resource costs. They leave complete parent-generator provenance unresolved. The project admits a candidate after it establishes the required license and rebuilds the exact circuit twice, or uses a documented independent generator. Test partially reproduced public designs as comparative controls. Keep optimizer reproduction, retained factorization data, and loader generation distinct in the provenance record. Require higher-than-34 metric and confirmation from the same frozen archive for a completed implementation. ## Independent critique

Astra 6.2 identified a two-component route worth screening first. Holding chemistry at 249.246M, an inverse-square-root cost of 58,000 and a DQI width of 880 with 316,123 executed Toffolis yield 34.13974 at four DQI errors per full draw. At eight errors the estimate is 34.01319. Nine errors take it below 34. The three-component budget provides more room for error variation. Both routes depend on achieving the proposed widths on complete circuits.

The review also checked orthogonal rotations of the public chemistry coefficient columns. These preserve the factorization's Gram tensor and offset contractions in exact arithmetic. The observed LCU norm is already close to the Gram lower bound. This limits the entire rotation class to roughly 17,075 cost savings, compared with the 4.246M reduction needed for 245M. Loader synthesis and changes to effective sparse support have more room under this budget. [Astra 6.2 critique](aggregate-above-34-independent-critique-26-11-2026-02-57-48.md)
