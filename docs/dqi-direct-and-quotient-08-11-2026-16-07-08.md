# Direct Forney alternative and quotient-field primitive

The direct alternative takes a supplied, possibly rescaled, square-free locator and the original syndrome bank. It computes `omega = sigma*S mod z^t`, evaluates `sigma`, `omega`, and `sigma'` at every `X_j^-1`, uses the 102-Toffoli subtower inverse to form `1/sigma'` at that position, applies the runtime-mask phase, and reverses the inverse and evaluator. The weight-zero constant locator is supported. Locator construction is outside this fixture.

Native tests use every supported weight at `(63,4)`, `(127,12)` and full locator degree `(70,70)`, independently generated errors, arbitrary nonzero locator scales, phase checks, input restoration and clean scratch. Measured fixture receipts are Q/T `195/14,625`, `387/31,097`, and `1,779/83,930`, respectively. The degree-35 fixture emission took 7.70s and 97,012 KiB peak single-process RSS, and produced a 27 MiB KMB; this is not process-tree PSS. Three focused WSL tests, Ruff and Pyright passed.

At `(4095,70)`, the fixed arithmetic formula is `108*35*36/2 + 227*4095 = 997,605` static Toffoli-like gates: 68,040 for evaluator forward/reverse and 929,565 for the scan. The full-position KMB was not emitted or verified. The degree-35, 70-position fixture declares 1,779 qubits. The only position-dependent native field maps are constant `gf2_imul` and `gf2_idiv`; exhaustive emission of all 4,095 nonzero constants shows each uses only its 12 input qubits. The remaining per-position allocations are structurally identical, so 1,779 is a structural width bound for the same supplied-locator scan over all 4,095 positions, not a measured full circuit.

If the separate 36-word virtual BM base, seven-bit age, eight-bit counter and conservative 722-bit indexed history remain live with this exact scan, the scan-time layout is bounded by 2,948 qubits before other locator work. At that width the 2x admission threshold against the matched reference is 1,180,320 total Toffoli-like gates, leaving 182,715 after the direct scan. A straightforward unrolling of the current 54-Toffoli polynomial products uses 1,890 discrepancy and 1,925 update products per direction, or 412,020 Toffoli-like gates in both directions before controls, inversions or shifts. Under that specific layout and static schedule the optimistic ratio is at most 1.675x, missing 2x. This is a conditional schedule estimate, not an impossibility proof: a different layout, arithmetic schedule, actual executed counts or repetition factors can change it. Receipt and assumptions are in `docs/evidence/dqi-direct-forney-20261108/`.

For the true reciprocal path, added a clean reversible monic quotient-product XOR with quantum modulus coefficients. It computes the full polynomial product into scratch, applies triangular monic long reduction without destroying the high coefficients, XORs the low remainder into an arbitrary target, then reverses reduction and product. Native tests for degrees 1, 2 and 3 cover random operands, nonzero targets, zero/reducible moduli, phase, clean scratch and composition. Degree 2 measured Q/T 135/648; degree 3 measured 207/1,620. Three WSL tests, Ruff and Pyright passed; receipts are in `docs/evidence/dqi-quotient-20261108/`.

Its cost is exactly `108*(2d^2-d)` Toffoli-like gates with the present 54-gate field product, or 260,820 at degree 35. It is a multiplication primitive, not an implemented reciprocal. For a fixed degree `w`, normalized locator `sigma` has monic reversal `p(x)=x^w*sigma(1/x)`; for a square-free locator split over GF(4096), the quotient is a product of fields and a unit `h` has inverse `h^4094`. A straightforward square-and-multiply chain uses 21 quotient products forward and 21 in reverse, projecting 10,954,440 Toffoli-like gates with this primitive, before locator or phase work. Specialized modular squaring and half-degree Euclid may improve that substantially. Fixed-degree monic reversal also does not by itself handle the data-dependent weights 0–35. No efficient general reciprocal, full decoder, full-size verifier result or admission is claimed.
