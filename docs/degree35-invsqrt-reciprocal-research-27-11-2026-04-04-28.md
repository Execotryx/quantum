## Native streamed reciprocal

For a fixed exponent and supplied threshold `(D + 1) / 2`, the binary restoring step sets `r' = 2r - qD`, where `q = [2r >= D]`. Odd `D` makes the low bit of `r'` equal to the quotient digit, so it can be phased as soon as it is produced. The inverse obtains `r = (r' + qD) / 2`. One extra digit implements nearest rounding at Q28. The serialized circuit matched the reciprocal phase, preserved the 31 live input wires, and cleared scratch for all 128 divisors with eight trajectories each.

The 29-step forward and inverse schedule used **49 declared qubits**, **1,740 Toffoli-like gates**, and **29 fine rotations**. The remainder and flag take 10 wires before adder workspace. Its Toffoli-like component alone costs 85,260. This rejects the tested restoring schedule. For a corrected numerator bit `b`, the analogous step is `r' = 2r + b - qD`, and the output bit is `b XOR (r' mod 2)`. Reversing a scaled phase circuit must preserve the injected bits or recover them at the same boundary. The measured KMB SHA-256 is `dfcad3a436ef555b78a9398d95ce9096d2513b1d09e2c8e3ca52ed6157204b03`. ## Creative Investigator and Astra review

Creative Investigator proposed forward-only division with deferred measurement repair for a classical dividend of 256. For a seven-bit address `a`, the first remainder is `512 - (257 + 2*a) = 255 - 2*a`. The seven-bit repair cannot clear the terminal remainder of the fused divider. Legal inputs 1,048,576 and 1,048,577 have the same address and denominator 257 but leave different encoded remainders, 384 and 8. The resulting repair for a measurement on bit 3 cannot be chosen from the reciprocal address alone.

Astra reduced the fused non-restoring update `R' = 2*R + b - D + 2*D*s`, with old sign `s = [R < 0]`, to a nine-bit signed remainder with implicit parity. The previous sign is `bit1(R') XOR (R & 1) XOR (1-b) XOR bit1(D)`. It can be recovered using Clifford operations once the old parity and incoming bit are available. The transformed arithmetic is one eight-bit signed add/subtract with a six-variable-bit source, a fixed high one, and one carry-in. The ordinary quotient digit is `[R' >= 0]`. Any phase emitted directly from the row sequence must use the live normalization scale `2^(k-10)` and account for the first digit, rounding digit, and endpoint corrections.

An independent numerical replay matched the transformed update and its inverse at every one of 29 steps on all 3,133,452 legal inputs. The resulting rounded quotient matched exact integer division on every input. [Compressed recurrence receipt](evidence/aggregate36-20261126/invsqrt-reciprocal-fused-sign-recurrence.json)
