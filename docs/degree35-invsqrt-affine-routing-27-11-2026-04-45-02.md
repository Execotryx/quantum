# Full-domain affine routing screen

The source-built 39-qubit inverse-square-root circuit needs 95 fewer charged gates to reach the 58,000 joint development allocation if the rotation and T charges are unchanged. The cloud screen tested whether the action of a controlled swap or Toffoli could be represented by affine functions of the live qubits at the same point. A valid replacement would preserve the width and remove one charged gate. [Joint cost screen](degree35-joint-cost-screen-27-11-2026-04-37-37.md) · [Control cost](evidence/aggregate36-20261126/invsqrt-checkpoint-return-control-native.json)

For a Toffoli, the product must lie in the affine span of untouched wires. A controlled swap toggles both targets by `control AND (left XOR right)`. Its replacement may also use the parity of the two targets, realized as an unconditional swap before applying the common affine update. This proof depends on the reachable states at that location. The scan stops at the first measurement-guarded value operation. Unconditional measurements before that point reset the measured qubit independently of the outcome. [Source screen](../tools/invsqrt_affine_routing.py) · [Assertion-based circuit optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00375)

The cloud VM screened **624 Toffolis and 107 controlled swaps** before line 10,972 of the pinned source. An 8,192-input sample admitted 35 possible substitutions. All 35 failed the exact affine-span check over **3,133,452** legal inputs. Membership on the full domain would imply membership on the sample, so the sample filter cannot discard an exact replacement in this family. The screen saved **zero** gates and emitted no candidate.
