# DQI evaluator and phase-only scan

Implemented the standalone exact phase-only scan for precomputed locator, evaluator and reciprocal coefficients. It walks roots `alpha**-j` using in-place constant multiplication, computes the three values, emits an 11-Toffoli zero test and 12 CCZs under each runtime mask, reverses the evaluation, and restores every coefficient. A reversible evaluator XORs the truncated locator-syndrome product into a target; it costs 54 times `t(t+1)/2` Toffoli-like operations with the existing 54-term tower multiplier. An ordinary polynomial extended-Euclid routine computes the derivative reciprocal classically; it rejects noninvertible derivatives and handles the constant locator as a no-phase case. It is not a reversible reciprocal circuit.

For a square-free locator, `Psi * sigma' = 1 (mod sigma)` implies `Psi(X) = 1/sigma'(X)` at every root `X`, so `omega(X) * Psi(X)` equals the Forney value. The pinned trace-dual basis satisfies `Tr(dual_i * e) = e_i`; by linearity, `Tr((sum mask_i * dual_i) * e)` equals the bit parity of `mask & e`. The emitted kernel directly forms the equivalent binary multiplication-tensor transpose conditioned on the runtime mask. Tests compare these identities with independent Forney recovery for each weight 0–35, including zero.

The (63,4) standalone scan has 143 declared qubits and 1,449 Toffoli-like operations; (127,12) has 287 qubits and 2,921 operations. Circuit receipts are in `docs/evidence/dqi-phase-scan-20261108/`. Standalone emissions took 3.37s / 40,608 KiB peak RSS and 3.73s / 45,652 KiB respectively, measured by `/usr/bin/time` including Python startup. Those are single-process RSS, not process-tree PSS or verifier timings. Native reduced-shape tests cover every supported reduced weight, input restoration and scratch cleanup; mutation tests detect a skipped position, wrong mask bit, and skipped transform reversal. The 45 focused WSL tests, Ruff, and Pyright passed.

These are development primitives. Locator and reversible reciprocal assembly, the matched full-size development verifier, error and cost admission, full 102,400-shot verification, and one-use confirmation remain open.

A limited reversible reciprocal is now implemented for degree-at-most-two locators: in characteristic two the derivative is the constant coefficient sigma_1, so the existing clean inverse-XOR yields Psi, including zero for the constant weight-zero locator. Its circuit contract assumes a square-free nonconstant locator; a non-square-free quadratic is outside that contract. A native (63,4) composition computes evaluator and reciprocal from locator and syndromes, scans all 63 runtime masks, and reverses both computations, with phase, input-restoration and clean-scratch checks at weights 0, 1 and 2. This composition costs 2,133 Toffoli-like operations and still requires a supplied locator. The general degree-35 reversible reciprocal remains open. All 46 focused tests, Ruff and Pyright passed.
